Special Tribute

In Memory of Abhya Luthra

We are very sad to share that Abhya Luthra is no longer with us. He passed away because of a serious illness.

He was the elder son of his mother, Mrs. Jakirot. His family and everyone who knew him are very sad because of his passing.

Abhya had a wish to start an NGO that could help people and children who need support. He wanted to provide free education and help to those who cannot afford it.

To respect his wish and keep his memory alive, AD Foundation was started as a free NGO.

Today, we are trying to continue his dream by helping people through education and support.

We will always remember Abhya Luthra and his wish to help others.

May his soul rest in peace.

With Respect,
AD Foundation

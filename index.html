Our Story

In Loving Memory of Avay Luthra

AD Foundation is a free NGO.

This NGO is made in the loving memory of Avay Luthra. Avay brought happiness to his family and people around him.

After Avay passed away, his mother Rakhi Luthra wanted to keep his memories alive. She wanted to do something good for children and society. Because of this dream, AD Foundation was started.

The main goal of AD Foundation is to help children get education and learn new skills for free. Here, children can learn English, Mathematics, Science, Computer, Graphic Design and Artificial Intelligence without any cost.

Every child has a dream. AD Foundation wants to help children achieve their dreams and build a bright future.

Today, this foundation is working with love, care and dedication in the memory of Avay Luthra.

"A small step for education, a big tribute to Avay Luthra."
